Our Journey

We are one of the leading bottling partners of The Coca-Cola Company in Kolkata. Founded in 1993, Diamond Beverages (P) Limited (DBPL) has maintained an indomitable market position ever since, now trusted by thousands of retailers and distributors in the city. We are involved in all aspects of beverage bottling, which include manufacturing, packaging, and distribution.
